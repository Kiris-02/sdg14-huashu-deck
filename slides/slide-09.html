Identify Responsible Authorities

2

Map Plan Boundaries

Delineate precise geographic coordinates and maritime zones (territorial seas, baseline, EEZ, specific estuaries or bays).

3

Complete National Questionnaire

Submit standardized statutory reporting forms to UNEP documenting plan legal status, participating sectors, and public consultation.

4

Classify Development Stage

Assign each management framework into one of four recognized maturity phases ranging from conceptual preparation to adaptive execution.

Four Official Implementation Phases
Phase 1: Initial Preparation
Scoping stakeholder priorities and establishing inter-ministerial taskforces.
DRAFTING
Phase 2: Plan Development
Zoning analysis, conflict mapping, and drafting statutory spatial allocations.
TECHNICAL MAP
Phase 3: Formal Adoption
Legally enacted into law or formally gazetted by cabinet / parliament.
LEGAL STATUTE
Phase 4: Adaptive Management
Active operational enforcement, funded monitoring, and iterative periodic review.
OPERATIONAL
Quality Assurance: UNEP and Regional Seas bodies run automated checks, manual review, and national registry cross-referencing. Missing national data are never imputed.
