Maturity Rubric Criteria

The plan has active regulatory enforcement, dedicated financial appropriations, and a built-in cycle of monitoring and iterative revision based on observed environmental response.

UNEP Verification Standards
  • Publicly verifiable legal gazette citation or statutory decree.
  • Identified institutional authority with maritime patrol enforcement.
  • Documented monitoring indicators and scheduled 5-year review revisions.
  • No imputation: missing national questionnaire data is recorded as null.
* UNEP Regional Seas coordinates automated checks and manual data validation.
