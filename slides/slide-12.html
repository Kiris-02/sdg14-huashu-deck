Indicator 14.3.1: Sampling stations & carbonate parameters

12 / 15
Station ALOHA · Hawaii
01 Representative Sampling Stations

Stations must observe frequently enough to characterize natural seasonal fluctuations and provide certified metadata allowing integration into the Global Ocean Acidification Observing Network (GOA-ON).

02 Two-Parameter Thermodynamic Rule

A single pH reading cannot resolve long-term acidification from diurnal respiration. Protocols mandate measuring at least two of four core parameters: pH, pCO₂, Dissolved Inorganic Carbon (DIC), or Total Alkalinity (TA).

03 Mandatory Auxiliary Metadata

Every pH submission must record water temperature, salinity, and hydrostatic depth. Without temperature and salinity, thermodynamic carbonate equilibrium dissociation constants cannot be computed.
