Indicator 14.3.1 Standard

Average marine acidity (pH) measured at an agreed suite of representative sampling stations, reported on the total scale (pH_T).

The Minimum "Two-of-Four" Rule

Because carbonate equilibrium is mathematically constrained, a complete chemical solution requires measuring at least two of four core parameters: pH, pCO₂, DIC, or Total Alkalinity.

Distinguishing Season from Secular Trend

Coastal pH naturally swings over daily and seasonal cycles due to photosynthesis and respiration. A single annual snapshot is useless; only high-frequency repeated observation detects the multi-decadal shift.
