Policy vs Reality
WHAT THE INDICATOR COUNTS

Policy Formulation & Legal Enactment

Indicator 14.2.1 officially recognizes sovereign institutional capacity when a formal marine management plan is gazetted or adopted.

✓ Statutory Existence: A published spatial plan with defined boundaries approved by government authorities.
✓ Jurisdictional Framework: Inter-ministerial coordination established on paper across fisheries and environment.
✓ Global Credit: Country is counted positively in the UN global database as having qualifying EBM arrangements.
CONFIRMS: The country possesses the legislative machinery to regulate marine areas.
THE ON-THE-WATER REALITY

Implementation Gaps & "Paper Parks"

Adopting a plan does not automatically restore fish stocks or protect corals if enforcement, financing, or ecological surveillance are missing.

⚠ The "Paper Park" Phenomenon: Marine areas protected in legislation but subject to illegal fishing and dredging due to zero patrol vessels.
⚠ Chronic Underfunding: Agencies lack operational budgets to conduct regular adaptive ecological monitoring.
⚠ No Biological Metric: Indicator does not record coral cover, biomass abundance, or nursery habitat recovery.
CRUCIAL CAVEAT: A plan is a prerequisite for protection, not proof of ecological health.
Source: UNSD Metadata for Indicator 14.2.1; UNEP; CBD Kunming-Montreal GBF [5–8].
Rule: Explicitly state that adopting a plan is not equivalent to successfully restoring an ocean ecosystem.
