Institutional Architecture

Governance for Target 14.1 operates from global custodians to national source control

06 / 15
Governance Continuum
GLOBAL CUSTODIAN LEVEL

UNEP & Global Programme of Action (GPA)

UNEP compiles global statistical indicators, manages quality assurance, and harmonizes national reporting across international ocean observing frameworks.

Core Mandate: Methodological custodian for Indicator 14.1.1.
Platform: GPA for protection from land-based activities.
REGIONAL BASIN LEVEL

Regional Seas Conventions & Action Plans

Shared marine basins (Mediterranean, Baltic, Caribbean, East Asian Seas) coordinate legally binding protocols to combat transboundary marine debris and river plumes.

Key Mechanism: 18 Regional Seas Programmes.
Role: Harmonizes localized sampling protocols across neighboring states.
NATIONAL REGULATORY LEVEL

Source-Control Policy Instruments

Monitoring reveals pollution patterns, but actual marine recovery requires domestic enforcement: bans on single-use plastics, wastewater treatment standards, and fertilizer limits.

Key Mechanism: Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR).
Domestic Levers: Agricultural discharge caps & municipal waste infrastructure.
Source: UNEP Regional Seas Programme; UNEP GPA for the Protection of the Marine Environment from Land-based Activities [1–4].
Rule: Note that monitoring alone does not fix the sea—governments require enforceable source control.
