▼ Intergovernmental Alignment

▼ Sovereign Enforcement
National & Subnational Level Implementation Anchor
National Ministries & Enforcement Authorities

Validates raw data submissions, legislates waste standards, funds municipal sanitation infrastructure, and enforces statutory discharge penalties.

Sovereign Source-Control Levers

Where physical intervention must occur to alter marine indicator trajectories:

Wastewater Treatment
Upgrading secondary and tertiary municipal sewage to strip bioavailable nitrogen and phosphorus before discharge.
Agricultural Runoff
Precision fertilizer subsidies, riparian buffer zones, and wetland restoration along major farming corridors.
Municipal Solid Waste
Mandatory closed-loop collection, leak-proof landfill management, and riverine trash boom interceptors.
Plastics Regulation
Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR), bans on problematic single-use items, and virgin polymer tax schemes.
The Governance Paradox: Marine observation only detects where pollution travels; sovereign land-based regulation is the only force capable of stopping it at the tap.
