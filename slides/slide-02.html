Integrated Policy Architecture

Three targets follow one connected chain of ocean risk

02 / 15
Causal Continuum
Phase 1 · Upstream Source Target 14.1

Pressure Reduction

Stops land-based contaminants before they enter the sea. Focuses on agricultural nutrient runoff causing coastal eutrophication and synthetic plastic debris density.

Domain:Source-to-Sea Catchments
Official Indicator:14.1.1 (Chl-a & Plastics)
Custodian Agency:UNEP
Phase 2 · In-Situ Habitat Target 14.2

Ecosystem Management

Builds ecological buffer capacity. Replaces fragmented sectoral exploitation with integrated spatial planning, marine protected areas, and active habitat restoration.

Domain:EEZ & Coastal Waters
Official Indicator:14.2.1 (EBM Plans)
Custodian Agency:UNEP / Regional Seas
Phase 3 · Global Water Column Target 14.3

Chemical Shift

Tracks planetary acidification caused by seawater absorbing atmospheric CO₂. Quantifies the continuous decline in pH and aragonite saturation for shell-forming organisms.

Domain:Global Water Column
Official Indicator:14.3.1 (Marine Acidity / pH)
Custodian Agency:IOC-UNESCO
The Systemic Chain: Reducing upstream pollution (14.1) protects ecosystem health (14.2), which provides coastal communities the resilience required to withstand unstoppable global acidification (14.3).
