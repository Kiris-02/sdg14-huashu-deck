Causal Continuum
Phase 1 · Upstream Source Target 14.1

Pressure Reduction

Stops land-based contaminants before reaching the coast. Focuses on agricultural nutrient runoff (eutrophication) and synthetic plastic debris density.

Domain:Source-to-Sea Basins
Key Indicator:14.1.1 (Chl-a & Plastics)
Custodian:UNEP
Phase 2 · In-Situ Resilience Target 14.2

Ecosystem Management

Builds ecological buffer capacity. Replaces fragmented sectoral exploitation with integrated spatial planning, marine protected areas, and active restoration.

Domain:EEZ & Coastal Waters
Key Indicator:14.2.1 (EBM Plans)
Custodian:UNEP / Regional Seas
Phase 3 · Planetary Chemistry Target 14.3

Chemical Shift

Tracks planetary-scale acidification driven by atmospheric CO₂ absorption. Quantifies long-term decline in seawater pH and aragonite calcification saturation.

Domain:Global Water Column
Key Indicator:14.3.1 (Average Marine pH)
Custodian:IOC-UNESCO
The Cascading Reality: If nutrient and plastic pressure (14.1) is not curbed, coastal ecosystem resilience (14.2) collapses, rendering marine habitats completely defenseless against ocean acidification (14.3).
