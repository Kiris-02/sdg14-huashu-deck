Indicator 14.2.1

The Formal Statistical Metric

"Number of countries using ecosystem-based approaches to managing marine areas (within national waters and exclusive economic zones)."

Binary State Logic (1 / 0)

Unlike biophysical chemical readings, national reporting evaluates administrative status: coded as 1 when qualifying ecosystem plans exist, and 0 when absent.

Twin Policy Anchors: ICZM & MSP

Qualifications require either Integrated Coastal Zone Management (ICZM) covering coastal zones, or Marine Spatial Planning (MSP) covering offshore EEZ waters.

Indicator 14.2.1 National Scoring Rules UNEP Custodian Methodology
