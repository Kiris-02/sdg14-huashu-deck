Management Architecture

✕ What Indicator 14.2.1 Does NOT Measure

It does not measure coral reef coverage, live fish biomass, benthic water clarity, or species extinction rates. A country can score highly on 14.2.1 while its coral reefs suffer catastrophic bleaching.

✓ What Indicator 14.2.1 DOES Measure

It measures the number of countries using ecosystem-based approaches (EBM) to manage marine areas through legally recognized spatial plans (ICZM, MSP, marine zoning).

⚡ The Practical Governance Rationale

Ecosystem outcomes take decades to manifest. Tracking governance maturity provides immediate multilateral accountability on whether sovereigns are establishing institutional capacity.
