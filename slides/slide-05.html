14.1.1 Measurement Architecture

Indicator 14.1.1 combines satellite observations with in-situ data

Two-Tier Observation System
LEVEL 1: GLOBALLY COMPARABLE REMOTE SENSING

Satellite Observations & River Models

Provides universally available baseline metrics for all maritime nations, ensuring no state is left unmonitored regardless of local sensor infrastructure.

✦
Chlorophyll-a Remote Sensing: High-frequency satellite ocean color data identifies monthly anomalies against 2000–2004 baselines in national EEZs.
✦
Global NEWS River Modeling: Estimates dissolved nitrogen, phosphorus, and silica discharges delivered to river mouths worldwide.
✦
Uniform Methodology: Enables impartial cross-country benchmarking coordinated directly by UNEP.
LEVEL 2: NATIONAL GROUND-TRUTH MONITORING

In-Situ Sampling & Beach Surveys

National authorities gather localized empirical data to ground-truth satellite readings and capture physical marine debris across diverse ocean compartments.

✦
Standardized Shoreline Transects: Field teams classify and count litter items per 100 m² of beach during seasonal tidal runs.
✦
Pelagic Manta Trawls: Surface nets quantify floating microplastic concentrations per square kilometer.
✦
Water Column CTD Rosettes: Oceanographic vessels record dissolved silica, nutrient ratios, and true in-situ dissolved oxygen.
Source: UN Statistics Division, Indicator 14.1.1 metadata; UNEP Global Environment Monitoring System [1–2].
Rule: Distinguish Level 1 global remote sensing from Level 2 in-situ national validation.
