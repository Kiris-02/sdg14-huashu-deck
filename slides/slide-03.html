Land-Based Upstream Priority

Over 80% of marine litter and nearly all coastal eutrophication originate from inland agricultural fertilizer runoff and untreated municipal waste.

Dual Chemical & Physical Targets

Target 14.1 mandates significant prevention across two distinct pollutant classes: biologically disruptive nutrients (N & P) and durable solid debris (plastics).

The Urgent Evaluation Question

With the 2025 milestone passed, the governance burden shifts from setting ambitious reduction goals to verifying if actual environmental influx is diminishing.
