Source-to-Sea Dynamics

The 2025 Mandate

By 2025, prevent and significantly reduce marine pollution of all kinds, in particular from land-based activities, including marine debris and nutrient pollution.

⏳
Expired 2025 Target Horizon:

Although the 2025 deadline has formally passed, global monitoring remains critical to determine whether agricultural runoff and plastic discharge are peaking.

The 80% Land-Origin Rule

Over 80% of marine pollution originates hundreds of kilometers inland via river catchments, municipal storm drains, wastewater plants, and agricultural fertilizer runoff.
