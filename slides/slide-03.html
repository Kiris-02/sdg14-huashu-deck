Source-to-Sea Dynamics

The 2025 Statutory Mandate

By 2025, prevent and significantly reduce marine pollution of all kinds, in particular from land-based activities, including marine debris and nutrient pollution.

⏳
Expired 2025 Target Horizon:

Although the 2025 deadline has formally passed, global monitoring remains critical to determine whether agricultural runoff and plastic leakage have peaked.

The 80% Land-Origin Reality

Over 80% of marine pollution originates hundreds of kilometers inland via river catchments, untreated wastewater outfalls, and agricultural fertilizer runoff.
