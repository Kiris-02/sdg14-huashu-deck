Global Ocean Governance Framework

SDG 14.1–14.3: How Ocean Progress Is Governed and Measured

Examining the continuum between international political ambition, quantitative empirical evidence, and cross-border institutional compliance across three interconnected marine crises.

DIMENSION 01
Targets
Agreed international policy mandates and timelines established by UN member states.
DIMENSION 02
Indicators
Standardized statistical variables (14.1.1, 14.2.1, 14.3.1) designed for uniform tracking.
DIMENSION 03
Measurement
Earth observation satellites, oceanic buoys, in-situ water sampling, and national reporting.
DIMENSION 04
Governance
Custodian agencies (UNEP, IOC-UNESCO), Regional Seas, and sovereign enforceable laws.
