Indicator 14.1.1 (a & b)
SUB-INDICATOR 14.1.1 (a)

Index of Coastal Eutrophication (ICEP)

Measures biogeochemical stress driven by excessive nitrogen (N) and phosphorus (P) delivery relative to silica (Si), triggering toxic algal blooms and hypoxic "dead zones".

Primary Proxy: Chlorophyll-a Anomaly Index
Driver Compound: N:P:Si Nutrient Imbalance
Impact Spectrum: Hypoxia & Ecosystem Suffocation
Spatial Scope: EEZ & Coastal Territorial Waters
Biological Productivity Shift: Satellite-derived chlor-a anomaly tracking compared against the 2000–2004 historical seasonal baseline.
SUB-INDICATOR 14.1.1 (b)

Plastic Debris Density

Measures the physical distribution and mass concentration of persistent synthetic polymers across shorelines, the ocean surface layer, the water column, and the benthos.

Beach Metric: Items / 100m Shoreline Transect
Floating Metric: Items / km² (Manta Trawl)
Deep Water: ROV & Seabed Video Sampling
Persistence: Centuries / Fragmentation to Micro
Physical Contamination Matrix: Multi-environment survey data capturing both macroscopic waste items and microscopic particles in ocean gyres.
Source: UNSD Metadata for Indicator 14.1.1 (Updated March 2025) [2].
Rule: Clarify that nutrient imbalance and solid plastic debris require separate monitoring systems.
