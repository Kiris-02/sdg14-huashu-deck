Indicator 14.1.1 is split into two distinct operational sub-indicators

Indicator 14.1.1 Structure
SUB-INDICATOR 14.1.1 (a)

Index of Coastal Eutrophication (ICEP)

Monitors abnormal biological productivity and phytoplankton biomass stimulated by dissolved nitrogen and phosphorus surplus relative to silica.

Primary Proxy: Chlorophyll-a Anomalies
Baseline Comparison: 2000–2004 Monthly Mean
Observation Method: Satellite Ocean Color (MODIS/Sentinel)
Biological Danger: Harmful Algal Blooms (HABs) & Anoxia
SUB-INDICATOR 14.1.1 (b)

Plastic Debris Density

Tracks the physical accumulation and dispersion of macro- and micro-plastics across coastal shorelines, surface pelagic gyres, and benthic seafloors.

Shoreline Standard: Items / 100 m² Beach Transect
Surface Trawl Standard: Items / km² Surface Waters
Seafloor / Column: ROV & Diver Visual Transects
Environmental Danger: Trophic Entanglement & Microplastic Ingestion
Source: UN Statistics Division, Indicator 14.1.1 metadata; UNEP Global Environment Monitoring System (GEMS/Ocean) [1–2].
Rule: Distinguish Level 1 global remote sensing from Level 2 in-situ national validation.
