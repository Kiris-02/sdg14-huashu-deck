Indicator 14.1.1 Architecture

Indicator 14.1.1 is split into two distinct operational sub-indicators

04 / 15
Sub-Indicator Structure
SUB-INDICATOR 14.1.1 (a)

Index of Coastal Eutrophication (ICEP)

Measures abnormal biological productivity and phytoplankton biomass stimulated by nitrogen and phosphorus surplus relative to silica, signaling coastal hypoxia.

Primary Satellite Proxy: Chlorophyll-a Anomalies
Reference Baseline: 2000–2004 Monthly Climatology
Observation Method: Ocean Color Satellites (MODIS / Sentinel-3)
Ecological Risk: Harmful Algal Blooms (HABs) & Dead Zones
SUB-INDICATOR 14.1.1 (b)

Plastic Debris Density

Quantifies the physical accumulation and dispersion of macro- and micro-plastics across coastal shorelines, surface pelagic waters, and deep benthic seabeds.

Shoreline Beach Standard: Items / 100 m² Beach Transect
Surface Trawl Standard: Items / km² Surface Waters
Water Column & Benthic: ROV, Trawl, & Diver Transects
Ecological Risk: Trophic Entanglement & Microplastic Toxicity
Source: UN Statistics Division, Indicator 14.1.1 metadata; UNEP GEMS/Ocean [1–2].
Rule: Explicitly separate nutrient pollution from marine plastic debris throughout Target 14.1.
