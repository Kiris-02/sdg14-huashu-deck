14.3.1 Data Pipeline

Indicator 14.3.1 standardizes annual marine acidity data globally

13 / 15
Data Harmonization
STEP 01 · OBSERVATION

Repeated In-Situ Sampling

Observing stations collect water column samples with sufficient temporal frequency to characterize seasonal cycles, tidal extremes, and upwelling pulses.

Frequency: Characterize annual cycle.
Parameters: 2 of 4 + T, S, Depth.
STEP 02 · PRIMARY QC

National Quality Control

Data providers calibrate sensor equipment using Dickson Certified Reference Materials (CRMs), run duplicate samples, and tag data with IOC standard quality flags.

Standard: Dickson Reference Materials.
Flags: 1 (Good), 2 (Suspect), 3 (Bad).
STEP 03 · SUBMISSION

Annual Portal Dispatch

Member States submit validated data packages via the SDG 14.3.1 Data Portal, supported by National Oceanographic Data Centres (NODCs) and IODE networks.

Format: Standardized CSV/NetCDF.
Hub: SDG 14.3.1 Data Portal.
STEP 04 · HARMONIZATION

Global Metric Calculation

IOC-UNESCO harmonizes datasets on the Total pH Scale and computes an equally weighted annual mean, standard deviation, and range for global reporting.

Metric: Annual Mean Total pH.
Reporting: UN SDG Progress Report.
Source: IOC-UNESCO, SDG 14.3.1 Data Portal Methodology and Metadata [1, 9–10].
Rule: Detail the 4-step pipeline: repeated observations, primary QC, annual submissions, global harmonization.
