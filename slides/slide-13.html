Quality Assurance Pipeline

Repeated, quality-controlled observations produce the annual value

13 / 15
Data Lifecycle
PHASE 1
Station / Sensor

Automated ISFET pH electrodes, spectrophotometric dye sensors, or Niskin bottle rosette casts collecting raw samples.

• High-frequency moorings • Research ship transects
PHASE 2
Laboratory & QC

Primary quality control using Certified Reference Materials (CRMs), calibrating electrode drift, and filtering outliers.

• Dickson seawater CRMs • Uncertainty quantification
PHASE 3
Annual Statistics

Aggregating time-series data into an equally weighted annual mean, factoring in seasonal cycle completeness and standard deviation.

• Seasonal cycle weighting • Full annual range (Min-Max)
PHASE 4
SDG Data Portal

Submission to IOC-UNESCO through National Oceanographic Data Centres (NODCs) for international peer review and registry.

• SDG 14.3.1 Data Portal • Open science repository

Required Submission Triad

Mandatory IOC-UNESCO Format
METRIC 01
Equally Weighted Annual Mean (pH_T)
METRIC 02
Standard Deviation (± σ dispersion)
METRIC 03
Annual Min–Max Extrema Range
