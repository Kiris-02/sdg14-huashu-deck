The Atmospheric Absorption Driver

The global ocean has absorbed approximately 25–30% of all anthropogenic CO₂ emissions since the industrial revolution, altering marine carbonate equilibrium on a planetary scale.

Scientific Cooperation Is the Target

Unlike Targets 14.1 and 14.2, no single coastal sovereign can isolate or fix its chemical regime alone. Scientific observation and data exchange are formally built into the target mandate.

Accurate Terminology: Becoming Less Alkaline

The ocean is not turning chemically acidic (pH remains > 7.0). Rather, mean surface seawater pH has dropped from ~8.25 to ~8.05—representing a ~30% increase in corrosive H⁺ ion concentration.
