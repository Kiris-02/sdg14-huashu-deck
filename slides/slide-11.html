The Atmospheric Absorption Driver

The global ocean has absorbed approximately 25–30% of anthropogenic CO₂ emissions since the industrial revolution, driving a planetary-scale shift in carbonate equilibrium.

Scientific Cooperation Is the Target Mandate

Unlike Targets 14.1 and 14.2, no single coastal sovereign can isolate or fix its chemical regime alone. Scientific observation and data exchange are formally embedded into the target wording.

Accurate Terminology: Becoming Less Alkaline

The ocean is not chemically acidic (pH remains > 7.0). Rather, mean surface seawater pH has dropped from ~8.25 to ~8.05—representing a ~30% increase in corrosive H⁺ ion concentration.
