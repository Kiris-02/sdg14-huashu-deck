Ecosystem Management

The 2020 Mandate

By 2020, sustainably manage and protect marine and coastal ecosystems to avoid significant adverse impacts, including by strengthening their resilience, and take action for their restoration.

Expired Target Horizon, Ongoing Priority

Like Target 14.1, the 2020 deadline has passed. Yet establishing ecosystem-based governance remains the centerpiece of national marine spatial planning worldwide.

The Ecosystem Approach Defined

A strategy for the integrated management of land, water, and living resources that promotes conservation and sustainable use in an equitable way, operating within planetary ecological boundaries.

The Governance Paradigm Shift Paradigm Shift

✕ Traditional Sectoral Silos

Fisheries ministries manage fish quotas in isolation; transport authorities permit shipping lanes; energy ministries lease offshore oil and gas blocks. Cumulative ecological stresses are ignored.

✓ Integrated Ecosystem Approach (EBM)

Marine spatial planning coordinates all maritime uses within ecological limits. Habitats (seagrass, coral reefs, mangroves) are protected as holistic life-support systems with active spatial zoning.

Core Principle: Ecosystem-based management does not manage ocean physics or biology; it manages human maritime activities to maintain the ocean's natural regenerative capacity.
Source: UN Statistics Division, Target 14.2; Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD) Ecosystem Approach [1, 5–8].
