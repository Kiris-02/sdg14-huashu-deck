Ecosystem Resilience
ORIGINAL STATUTORY DEADLINE: 2020 (PASSED)

Holistic Ecosystem Resilience

Shift from managing individual economic sectors in silos to protecting whole biomes so oceans remain biologically productive and climatically resilient.

Integrated Coastal Zone Management (ICZM)

Unifies terrestrial and coastal administrative bodies to manage the critical land-sea transition zone against shoreline erosion and habitat degradation.

Marine Spatial Planning (MSP)

A public process of analyzing and allocating the spatial and temporal distribution of human activities in marine areas to achieve ecological and economic objectives.

Traditional Siloed Approach

Ecosystem-Based Management (EBM)
