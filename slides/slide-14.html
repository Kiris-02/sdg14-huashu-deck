Institutional Networks
UN CUSTODIAN AGENCY

IOC-UNESCO

Leads global ocean science, sets methodological standards for Indicator 14.3.1, and manages the International Oceanographic Data and Information Exchange (IODE).

Role: Global statistical compiling agency.
Hub: SDG 14.3.1 Data Portal & IODE.
SCIENTIFIC OBSERVING NETWORK

GOA-ON Network

The Global Ocean Acidification Observing Network links hundreds of researchers worldwide, standardizing sensor calibrations and deploying 'GOA-ON in a Box' low-cost kits to developing states.

Capacity: Pier-to-peer scientist mentoring.
Toolkit: Low-cost spectrophotometric kits.
GLOBAL POLICY LINKAGE

UNFCCC & Paris Agreement

Local ocean acidification data feeds directly into the UNFCCC Ocean and Climate Change Dialogue, grounding Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs) in empirical ocean chemistry.

Pillar: Mitigation of global CO₂ emissions.
Outcome: Ocean-climate integrated governance.
Source: IOC-UNESCO Ocean Acidification programme; GOA-ON; UNFCCC Ocean Dialogue [1, 9–10].
Rule: Connect local pH sampling to global scientific coordination and UNFCCC climate negotiations.
