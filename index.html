Speaker 5 · Target 14.3
11 / 15
PLANETARY ACIDIFICATION

A Global Shift in Seawater Chemistry

The ocean does not become acidic (pH remains > 7.0); rather, it becomes less alkaline as surface water absorbs 25–30% of anthropogenic atmospheric CO₂.

Mandate: Scientific cooperation is formally written into the target itself.
+30%
Hydrogen Ion Surge

Average ocean pH has dropped from 8.25 to 8.05 since the industrial revolution, depleting carbonate ions needed for shells.

Open-Ended Horizon: No fixed statutory deadline IOC-UNESCO Ocean Acidification Programme
